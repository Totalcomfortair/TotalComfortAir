Maintenance

Small checks. Better comfort.

Simple, safe maintenance habits to keep your HVAC system running smoothly.

🧹

Check your filter

Inspect it regularly and replace it when dirty, following your equipment instructions.

🌬️

Keep airflow clear

Make sure supply and return vents remain free of furniture and other obstructions.

🌳

Watch the outdoor unit

Keep visible debris and vegetation away. Do not open electrical panels.

🌡️

Check thermostat

Verify mode and temperature settings before assuming equipment failure.

📅

Seasonal service

Professional maintenance helps catch issues before they become breakdowns.
